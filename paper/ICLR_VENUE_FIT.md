### Key risks at ICLR:
1. Reviewers may expect transfer to standard benchmarks (GAIA, SWE-bench, etc.)
2. Synthetic-only core evidence is a common rejection reason
3. N=16/14 for LLM/curated experiments is small by ICLR standards
