## Justification

The primary title captures the core finding (better retrieval can lead to worse reasoning), uses a colon structure common in top ML venues, and is neither overclaimed nor undersold. "Auditing" signals methodological rigor. "Intermediate Artifacts" is precise without being jargon-heavy.
