# Stage 4A — Representational Regime Revision

## Scientific Thesis

> Can ASAR recognize when the entire current representational regime is inadequate,
> construct a better representation, and empirically demonstrate that the new
> representation is superior — while controlling false revision?

## Hypotheses (all PROSPECTIVE)

- **SD-H11**: Detection of regime inadequacy with low false-revision rate
- **SD-H12**: Generation of correct missing structure
- **SD-H13**: Validated regime transitions improve held-out performance
- **SD-H14**: Active experiment design identifies better regime efficiently

## Conditions

- **ASAR**: Detect → Generate → Validate (3-step structured pipeline)

## Primary Metrics

- True regime detection rate (regime-failure worlds)
- False revision rate (negative + decoy controls)
